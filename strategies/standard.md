# standard — one pass, no scaffold

```yaml
name: standard
mode: prefix
passes: 1
cost: 1x
applies_to: any-tier
prompt_prefix: ""
```

**The default, and the right answer for most dispatches.** A strategy is scaffolding around a
run, and scaffolding around work that did not need it is paid noise — the same law as effort:
spend where the work is judgement, not everywhere. The selector falls back here whenever it
does not positively recognise a shape that earns more (`selector.md`).
