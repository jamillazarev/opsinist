# Competitors — the register the monitoring feeds

**The stock shape of a competitive register** (`docs/COMPETITORS.md`). One row per competitor,
and **every cell that can age carries its check-date** — a price, a tier, a claim about their
roadmap: past its recheck it is `unknown`, not fine, and the freshness checker reads this file
like any register. **Filled by research-typed work, fed by the monitoring automation**
(`catalogue.md` → Competitive monitoring), **read by** a spec's *Why now*, a positioning
brief, and the decide loop's criteria — never a shelf document.

| Who | Stage/outcome (live · pivoted · dead — dated) | Positioning, in one line | Price (fetched, dated) | Moat, with evidence | Where we differ | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| {{name}} | {{live · **or pivoted/dead, with when and the stated reason — a corpse's cause of death is evidence, not trivia**}} | {{their own words, quoted — not our caricature of them}} | {{$X/mo · checked YYYY-MM-DD}} | {{frame named (7 Powers / moat taxonomy) **and the evidence**: "switching costs — migrations take weeks, their forum says so" — a moat claim with no evidence is positioning copy}} | {{the one-line answer a sales page needs — ours, so `judgement` is an honest rung here}} | {{url or file#anchor (sha:…, checked …)}} |

**Rules this register lives by:**

- **Their claims are quoted, never paraphrased into strawmen** — a competitor described worse
  than they describe themselves is a register that loses arguments in public.
- **A gap is a row too**: a competitor nobody has profiled is written with empty cells and a
  `verify` line, because an empty register and a complete-looking one read the same from
  outside.
- **The watch feeds triage, not the register directly** — a detected change (pricing page
  moved, changelog shipped) lands as triage with the diff, and a person accepts it into a row:
  the register is a curated surface, not a scrape dump.
- **Comparative content cites this register** — a "vs" page, a sales answer, a launch post: one
  home for the facts, so two artefacts never carry two different prices for the same rival.
