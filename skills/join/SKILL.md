Take over a repository that already exists. Guest or successor is **read from the ground, not asked**; the audit comes **before anything is touched**; findings arrive as **one list, not one per message**, each **blocking or deferrable** with the consequence named; deferrable ones land in `LATER.md` with a revisit trigger that is a moment, not a date; **nothing is fixed before the owner has seen the list**.
